# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We aim to keep participation in Triage welcoming and respectful. Be considerate
of different backgrounds and experience levels, give constructive feedback,
and focus discussion on the work.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Project
maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation
when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers using the
contact method listed in the repository hosting service. Do not include
private information in a public issue.
