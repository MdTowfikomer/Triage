## 1. Product purpose

Triage turns short spoken thoughts into a private, categorized inbox.

## 2. Primary user

Busy solo professionals capturing and reviewing thoughts on phones and laptops in short sessions.

## 3. Principles

1. Suppression beats a wrong save: confidence below 0.1, empty calls, and invalid dates demand review.
2. Raw speech belongs to this device: original transcripts never enter API payloads.
3. The queue is the product: no decorative charts or dashboard metrics.
4. One capture, two taps: the voice capsule carries recording, processing, and confirmation.
5. Quiet chrome, meaningful color: the supplied Linear/Raycast palette and category accents are binding.

## 4. Success metric

A user can capture a thought, interrupt its three-second save, or act on a filtered inbox without navigation detours.

## 5. Out of scope

- Native FFI capture until platform integration is verified.
- Audio uploads and local notifications.
- Pretending deterministic text redaction is Desert Ant model integration.
- Generic Material styling, marketing panels, and fabricated metrics.
